About KITAMAC

Sport with a purpose.

KITAMAC Taekwondo Fitness & Leadership Centre is a youth-focused sports and community-development organization based in Aba, Abia State, Nigeria.

We combine Taekwondo, fitness, leadership education, mentorship, personal-safety awareness and community outreach to help children, adolescents and young people develop discipline, confidence, responsibility and positive leadership.

CAC BN-4091816 Aba, Abia State Youth Development

Our Vision

To build a generation of disciplined, confident, responsible and safety-conscious young people who contribute positively to their communities.

Our Mission

To use sport, leadership, education and community action to empower young people, promote safer environments and encourage responsible citizenship.

Safeguarding Commitment

Every child deserves dignity, respect and care.

KITAMAC promotes safe and respectful environments during its programmes and outreach activities.

We encourage concerns involving abuse, exploitation, violence or other forms of harm to be brought to appropriate authorities or qualified protection professionals.
Our role: KITAMAC supports awareness, prevention, responsible reporting and appropriate referral. We do not replace law-enforcement, medical, legal or specialist psychosocial services. Serious protection concerns should be referred to competent authorities and qualified service providers.
